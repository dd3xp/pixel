# Citation audit — `paper_assets/draft_full.md`

Every `[cite: ...]`, `[cite? ...]` and `[TODO cite ...]` marker in the draft was
extracted with its surrounding sentence and checked against primary sources
(arXiv abstract pages, conference proceedings, publisher records). A key counts
as VERIFIED only when the located paper actually supports the claim made in the
sentence where it appears — not merely when a paper of a similar name exists.

**Totals: 43 keys + 5 `[TODO cite]` items. 35 keys VERIFIED, 7 MISMATCH,
1 UNRESOLVED. All 5 TODO items resolved.**

`paper_assets/refs.bib` defines 45 entries: the 35 verified keys, the 7
mismatched keys (the underlying works are real and citable once the prose is
fixed; each carries a warning comment), and 3 new keys for the TODO items
(`Ho2020`, `Li2022blip`, `OpenGameArt`). The unresolved key has no entry.

The draft itself was not edited.

## Notes on placeholder keys

The 14 `[cite? ...]` keys were flagged as possible fabrications. Thirteen of
them resolve to real, locatable works — the invented-looking names
(`SGGBR2026`, `IG2025`, `SSG2026`, `CDG2026`, `S2Guidance2025`,
`InSituAG2025`, `TVCFG2025`, `SWG2024`, `Koulischer2025`) are all abbreviations
of genuine 2024–2026 guidance papers, verified against their arXiv abstract
pages. Four of them nonetheless need prose fixes (`InSituAG2025`, `SSG2026`,
`PixelOE2024`, `SpriteSheetDiffusion2024`, plus `diffusers`), and one —
`PixDiffPIG2025` — could not be verified at all.
